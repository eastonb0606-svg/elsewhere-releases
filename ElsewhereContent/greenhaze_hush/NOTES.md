## What is built
**The Greenhaze** (`elsewhere:greenhaze`): dimension type with fixed golden-hour sun (sun_angle attribute), lavender fog, low clouds (180); overworld noise with 5 biomes (Kush Canopy, Sativa Highlands, Indica Hollows, Hazebottom Swamp, Munchie Groves) with the doc's sky/fog/water/foliage colours, particles, music and custom mob spawns. Structures (code-built, one structure type `elsewhere:greenhaze_hush_built`): Great Hotbox, Bonfire Terrace, Sesh Huts (Budtender + chest), Lucid Springs, Return Rifts (~1 per 592 blocks, beacon beam, stub of the core's piece).
- Haze meter (`Haze`, attachment `elsewhere:haze`, synced, HUD bar): +1%/20 s capped at 60% in the Greenhaze, vents push it up, Lucid Lilies/Lucid Water bring it down; Frond set / Hotbox Visor block unchosen contact highs. With Green Leaf loaded the haze feeds Green Leaf's high through reflection (`GreenLeafBridge`, no compile dependency, falls back silently); without it the meter turns into mild Slowness/Nausea.
- Blocks: Kushwood set (log, wood, stripped x2, planks, stairs, slab, fence, gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate, leaves), Giant Fern (4-part), Haze Vent, Lucid Lily, Munchfruit Bush, Trichome Quartz Ore/Block, Haze Resin Block, Hotbox Altar, Snack Altar, Hotbox Vent, Vent Lever.
- Items: Haze Resin, Hazeborn Bud, Lucid Water, Munchie Bacon, Munchfruit, Hotbox Lantern, Trichome Quartz, Toad Slime, Shade Wisp, Trichome sword/pickaxe/axe/shovel/hoe (Slowness on hit), Frond hood/cloak/leggings/sandals, Everflow Bong, Hotbox Visor, Cloud Jar, Endless Snack Pouch, Couchlock Club, Grumblegut Tusk, Music Disc "Four Twenty", spawn eggs.
- Mobs: Lazy Gorger (naps, steals food from munchie players, eats dropped food), Munchie Boar (smells food, charges, knocks food out of your hand), Toke Toad (tongue-eats small mobs, lick for a strain hit / 10% Nausea), Hotbox Smog (slime-like 16/8/4 HP, engulfs: haze + Slowness), Paranoia Shade (only rendered/targeting at Baked 60%+), Bud Bomb (Percolax minion), Budtender (trader).

**The Hush** (`elsewhere:the_hush`): no sky, dense black-teal fog (fog end 56), ambient light 0, beds explode (nether bed rule); caves-preset 3D noise in deepslate, 5 biomes (The Murmur, Echo Shafts, Ossuary Galleries, Amethyst Choir, Drowned Silence) with sculk features and custom spawns. Structures: Listening Engine (Auris arena), Choir Geode (Lithara arena, with **the Hush's only exit** — a return rift in a sealed chamber under the black lake), Silent Monasteries (wool-lined, Mute Archivist, Resonance can't rise), Hanging Undercity (loot). No Return Rifts (user decision 2).
- Resonance meter (`Resonance`, attachment `elsewhere:resonance`, synced, waveform HUD bar): fed by vanilla game events through a `ServerLevel.gameEvent` mixin (`HushNoise` bus): sprint +1/s, jump +2, mine +3, hit +4, eat +5, chest +6, Green Leaf cough +25 (35% of Green Leaf uses in the Hush); decays 2/s sneaking, 4/s on wool/hushweave; Hushcloak x0.5, full Hushweave x0.25, Muted x0, Sculk Censer absorbs, Echo tools mine silently, Choristers' hymn masks. 50%: Echo Stalkers home in; 100%: a Warden digs out (Auris near the Listening Engine).
- Blocks: Hushstone set (stone/stairs/slab/wall, polished, bricks + stairs/slab/wall), Hushweave + carpet, Echo Crystal Ore/Block, Sculk Lantern (pulses with nearby noise), Ossuary Bone bricks set, Tympanum Membrane, Choir Pillar.
- Items: Echo Crystal, Resonant Shard, Hush Membrane, Sonar Horn, Sculk Censer, Silence Draught (+ `elsewhere:muted` effect), Tuning Fork of Auris (thrown decoy), Choir Vestment, Cantor's Bell Shard, Resonant Tuning (offhand: Resonance boss bar), Echo sword/pickaxe/axe/shovel/hoe, Hushweave hood/Hushcloak/Hushstep Greaves/boots, Music Disc "Hush", spawn eggs.
- Mobs: Echo Stalker (blind, hunts the noise bus, teleports to loud events), Shrieker Swarm (shrieks at noise, +8 Resonance), Ossuary Chorister (hymn masks noise, +20 on kill), Catalyst Mimic (shell, opens and fires Levitation bullets; no shulker shells), Mute Archivist (trader: Sonar Horn, Silence Draught, Swift Sneak book, disc).

**Bosses** (all `BossEntity` stub framework: HP-threshold phases with 2 s invulnerable transitions, boss bar with attack-name flash, overpower guard, lethal-hit guard mixin, arena seal + snapshot/restore, 30 s drop-out, party HP scaling, per-player loot with first-kill guarantee, telegraph decals):

Minions Bud Bombs, Paranoia Shades. Summon: sneak-use the Hotbox Altar with a Green Leaf joint/blunt/vape, or 4 Haze Resin without Green Leaf.
- Grumblegut (360/10/2): Gulp Charge with crumb-line decal, Munch + Hunger III, Snack Sniff eats ripe fruit trees (5%), dropped food baits it (chew 3 s, 1%, x1.5); phase 2 fake paranoia sounds, rotating smoke columns, Puff-Puff-Pass ring (sneak next to an ally to split); phase 3 Couch Slam (+6 armor, slower), Yawn vacuum + chomp, naps (x2) unless food is held near. - Signature gear (stub, core-owned): Challenger's Cleaver (I), Phasewalker Greaves (III, double-tap sneak dash), Echo-Forged Blade (V).
