# verdigris_ironpeak — The Verdigris Sea, Ironpeak, Verdigrim, Galvanox, Hrolf Grimhorn, Thurgrim

Dev mod id `elsewhere_verdigris_ironpeak`; every registry id, asset and data file is in the `elsewhere` namespace.
Build: `JAVA_HOME=S/jdk/jdk-25.0.5+8/Contents/Home ./gradlew build` (jar in `build/libs`, copy in `dist/`).
Self-test: `./gradlew runServer` (or `S/desk.sh <this folder> runServer`): logs `[selftest] PASS/FAIL` lines and a total, then exits. Port 25631.
Screenshots: `./gradlew runClientGameTest` → `screenshots/`.

### Hrolf Grimhorn, Champion of the Proving Grounds · Tier III · `hrolf` (my design for the dimension stub)
- **Arena / summon:** *The Proving Grounds* (~Y 700): a round shelf colosseum (radius 14) with iron-bar rails on three sides and an open cliff edge to the south, 4 clan-banner posts. Sound the **Challenge Horn** at the north gate; Hrolf rides in on Grimhorn.
- **Entities:** `elsewhere:hrolf` (rider, vindicator ×1.5, 0.9 × 2.9) and `elsewhere:grimhorn` (war goat ×3, 2.7 × 3.9). Both implement `Enemy`.
- **Stats:** rider 300 base HP, armor 12, toughness 4; Grimhorn 180 base HP, armor 10, toughness 2, knockback resistance 1. Combined 480 base / ~734 effective. Phases run on combined health.
- **Top hit:** Grimhorn's ram 44 raw ≈ 8.7 HP (44%), from a 1.5 s pawing wind-up with a yellow charge line; the ram launches you along its path (toward the open edge if you stood there).
- **Phases:**
  1. **Mounted (100–50%).** Grimhorn paws (1.5 s, yellow line), then rams straight across the arena; a wall (iron bars) impact stuns it 3 s. Hrolf swings from the saddle (14 raw). Slow Falling is removed inside the arena.
  2. **Duel (50–20%).** Horn blast dismount (Slowness II 4 s within 12 blocks, push). Overhead axe: 1 s yellow→red crack line, 42 raw along it. Shield-break kick against a blocking target: 20 raw, shield disabled 5 s. War-cry: 2 Ironclan Raiders (25 s cooldown, cap 2 + 2/extra player). Grimhorn keeps ramming whoever is closest.
  3. **Last Rank (20–0%).** If Grimhorn lives, Hrolf remounts and they charge every 4 s; if Grimhorn is dead, Hrolf berserks (moves and attacks ~25% faster, takes 20% more damage). Killing the goat early is the counterplay.
- **Drops:** Avalanche Horn (guaranteed), Champion's Belt (first kill, then 35%), 2–4 Skyiron, Tier III signature; sets clan rank 5 (Quartermaster rank-5 trades, Ironclan Raiders bow to you, Thurgrim's drum answers).
- **Counterplay:** sidestep the yellow line, bait rams into the rails, read the crack line, drop your shield when he lines up the kick.
